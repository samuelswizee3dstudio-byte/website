## When you place an order

Payment is handled by **Stripe**, not by us. You type your card details on
Stripe's own page — they never touch this website.

Stripe collects your name, email address, phone number, delivery address and
card details, and shares your name, email, phone number, address and order
details with us so we can make your order and get it to you. We ask for a phone
number so we can reach you if email does not get through.

If you choose delivery, we give your name and address to **Royal Mail** so they
can deliver the parcel. We do not share it with anyone else. If your order includes a
personalised item, the name or word you typed is part of the order.

We keep order records for six years because HMRC requires it. Stripe's own
privacy policy is at [stripe.com/gb/privacy](https://stripe.com/gb/privacy).

**Legal basis:** performing our contract with you, and our legal obligation to
keep business records.

## When you use the contact form

The form is handled by **Netlify**, our website host. Your name, email address
and message are emailed to us and stored in Netlify for
`[[REPLACE: 90]]` days, then deleted.

**Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in answering your question.

## Cookies and storage

We do not use any analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

Your shopping basket is stored in your own browser, on your own device, using
local storage. It never reaches our servers until you press checkout. Clearing
your browser data empties your basket.

Stripe sets its own cookies on its checkout page to process the payment and
prevent fraud.

## Who else sees your data

Only Stripe (payments) and Netlify (hosting and the contact form). We do not
sell, share or rent your details to anyone.

## Children

Our things are made for children, but orders should be placed by an adult. We do
not knowingly collect data from children.

## Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to
delete it — though we have to keep order records for six years. Use the
[contact form](/contact).

If you are not happy with how we handle your data you can complain to the
Information Commissioner's Office at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk).
